Privacy Notice

How InfoNext UK handles your information

Last updated: 24 September 2026

We only ask for information that helps us understand and deliver the service. Please do not send unnecessary passport copies, bank information, passwords or other sensitive documents in an initial enquiry.

1. Who is responsible for your information

InfoNext UK is responsible for the personal information it receives and uses for its information, research and support services.

Privacy contact: hello@infonextuk.co.uk.

2. Information we may collect

  • your name and contact details;
  • your country or location;
  • the question, circumstances, goal and deadline you provide;
  • messages and correspondence with us;
  • documents or information you later provide for an agreed service;
  • payment and transaction records when paid services are introduced;
  • records of services purchased and work delivered.

3. How we collect information

We may receive information directly from you through WhatsApp, email, website forms, video calls, social media or other agreed communication channels. We may also receive information from a professional you have authorised us to communicate with.

The website enquiry form currently prepares the information you enter as a structured email addressed to InfoNext UK. The website itself does not store the completed form. Your own email provider and our email provider will process the message under their respective terms and privacy arrangements.

4. Why we use your information

  • to understand your enquiry and confirm the appropriate service;
  • to provide research, information, administrative support or an action plan;
  • to communicate with you about the service;
  • to coordinate with an authorised professional where you have asked us to do so;
  • to manage payments, accounting, complaints and business records;
  • to comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

5. Our lawful bases

Depending on the situation, we may rely on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, or our legitimate interests in running and protecting the business.

If special-category information becomes necessary for a particular service, we will only use it where there is an appropriate lawful condition. Please do not send special-category information unless it is genuinely relevant and requested.

6. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information.

Where necessary, we may share information with service providers that support our communications, website, payments or administration, or with an appropriately authorised professional where you have asked us to coordinate your matter. We may also disclose information where required by law.

7. Third-party platforms

If you choose to contact us using WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or another third-party service, that platform will process information under its own privacy terms. We recommend reviewing the platform's privacy information if you have concerns about how it processes data.

8. International transfers

Some communications, hosting or technology providers may process information outside the UK. Where this applies, we will use providers and safeguards appropriate to the service and applicable data-protection requirements.

9. How long we keep information

Routine enquiries that do not become paid customer matters are normally kept for no longer than 90 days unless there is a valid reason to retain them longer.

Customer and transaction records may be kept for up to six years after the service ends where this is reasonably necessary for accounting, contractual, legal or dispute-resolution purposes. We will delete information earlier where it is no longer needed and there is no legal reason to retain it.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit access to customer information, use appropriate account security and avoid collecting more data than we need. No online communication method is completely risk-free, so customers should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

11. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to ask for access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis used.

To make a privacy request, email hello@infonextuk.co.uk.

12. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we use your information, contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office where applicable.

13. Cookies and analytics

We do not currently use advertising or marketing analytics cookies on this website. If that changes, this notice and any required cookie controls will be updated before those tools are introduced.

14. Changes to this notice

We may update this Privacy Notice when our services, technology providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this website.
